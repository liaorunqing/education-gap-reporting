# Conditions for educational-gap reporting

The practical decision is what evidence a monitoring report can responsibly state. It is not whether to allocate services to a nativity group or attribute attainment to migration.

| Actual information before selection | Candidate procedure | Must be checked locally |
|---|---|---|
| Eligible addresses/households and a probability sampling design | Direct probability-sample estimates, if the target and required denominators are estimable | Frame coverage, group sample sizes, nonresponse and compatible variance estimation; fixed denominators in this benchmark are an extra assumption |
| Above plus reliable weighted age–sex–nativity totals and historical cell rates | Historical-cell/model-assisted control | Totals must correspond to the actual target and time; estimated margins add uncertainty |
| Above plus current linked adult background and fixed predictions for every eligible unit | HGB difference estimates and proportional allocation | Roster completeness, lawful linkage, current membership, historical-to-current prediction drift |
| Above plus household risks or a budgeted pilot | Fine working Neyman, two-wave and risk-targeted designs | Selection probabilities, pilot budget, household composition change and conditional design/variance compatibility |

The observed 88.7% MSE reduction compares the last setting with HGB-assisted random sampling under the same fixed-frame assumptions. It does not establish that an address-only organisation can obtain that gain, or isolate the causal value of one demographic field. Known margins and a linked roster are different resources.

An existing panel offers a plausible future setting for updating adult qualifications: its previous wave may contain age, sex, country of birth and household relationships. Before applying this benchmark one would need an audit of current entrants/departures and recontact eligibility, measured nonresponse, consent/linkage permissions, and the accuracy of current weights. Understanding Society documentation supplies a workflow example, not those validations or a new analysis of UKHLS participant data.

For a report, fix the population, age–sex reference and reporting resolution before seeing the estimates. Show the estimate and interval. A 95% interval wholly above +2pp or below −2pp supports the corresponding directional statement; one wholly inside [−2pp,+2pp] supports confinement to that resolution; other intervals retain judgment. Publish reporting yield and wrong reports both per attempt and per produced report. No statement implies causal or social equality.

The eight states are purposively selected, and the original ACS uncertainty is distinct from added-sample uncertainty. The SC failure and unfavorable learned-risk comparisons remain visible. No current auxiliary roster, field implementation, monetary saving, independent degree verification or general national performance is established by this release.

## SIPP empirical feasibility audit

Public 2024-to-2025 SIPP records now provide a concrete prior-background example. After planned panel rotation is excluded, the defined baseline contains 22,586 adults in 13,054 prior households. There are 13,845 valid later matches and 7,938 fully observed prior clusters. That documents historical information for recontact; it does not establish complete follow-up. New persons, household splits and inconsistent background/qualification records are audited. Household attainment questions are proxy-reported in the demographics module; dependent interviewing is documented more generally in SIPP. These are not verified independent degrees.

The pilot fixes historical weights, groups and age/sex reference, then evaluates only fully observed prior clusters. This future-known subset cannot be treated as a preselection frame. Gains in its sampling precision do not remove attrition or validate a resident-population report. Official longitudinal weights exist for two-year cohorts but are constructed after follow-up; this study does not present them as available in advance. The public-record missing-outcome bounds are hypothetical sensitivity calculations for a defined binary baseline-cohort outcome, not confidence intervals or verified current-eligibility bounds.

Timing qualification: the 2024 SIPP public file was released on 24 July 2025, after the February–June 2025 follow-up fieldwork. Earlier-reference final weights are not thereby proven available before selection. Historical responses can precede recontact internally, but this retrospective public-file pilot does not validate an as-of operational extract or preselection availability of final weights.
